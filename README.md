**Role: Data Visualization & ML Optimization Support**

A collaborative project between two engineering teams (Data Collection & Machine Learning) to simulate and predict energy outcomes.

**The Ecosystem:**
*   **[ElectricSim-Data](https://github.com/ivanpolanis/ElectricSim-Data):** Collects and stores raw energy usage data.
*   **[ElectricSim-ML](https://github.com/ramirocabral/ElectricSim-ML):** A Neural Network trained on the collected data to predict future states.

*   **Infrastructure:** Configured and allocated high-performance hardware resources to enable faster iteration cycles during the Neural Network training phase.
